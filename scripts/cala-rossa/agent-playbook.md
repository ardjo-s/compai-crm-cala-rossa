# Cala Rossa prospecting playbook

Use this playbook inside the `Entré Business Cala Rossa` workspace only.

## Objective

Prepare evidence-backed outreach for qualified luxury villa operators in Cala Rossa.

Never send outreach. Never publish media. Never buy enrichment. Stop at a review-ready draft.

## Record model

- Company: the buyer account or operator.
- Deal: one exact villa or one clearly labelled inventory class.
- Task: one human-gated preparation sequence for the best pilot at each account.
- Contact: create only after a public source verifies the person's identity and decision-making role.

## Priority order

1. Work P1 accounts by account rank.
2. Continue with P2 accounts after every P1 route has a verified outcome.
3. Use P3 for route research only.
4. Leave Nurture records untouched until their media or commercial signal changes.

## Preparation sequence

1. Verify the exact property and operator identity from first-party pages.
2. Verify the public business route. Do not access private personal contact data.
3. Recheck rental price with exact stay dates, occupancy, fees and taxes.
4. Inspect the existing video and 3D fields before choosing the offer.
5. Request written media authorization and camera originals before generating a proof.
6. Build only a private, watermarked, 20-second proof.
7. Label generative spatial output as a concept. Never label it as a factual property twin.
8. Require systematic overlapping capture for a factual exploratory 3D experience.
9. Draft one message with the verified recipient, evidence and pilot property.
10. Stop for human approval before any send or publication.

## Required evidence

- Exact property URL.
- Exact operator route.
- Rental price basis or a visible unavailability reason.
- Visible image count and delivery limits.
- Existing property video status.
- Existing factual 3D status.
- Reuse-rights status.
- Unresolved identity or media gap.

## State changes

- `Not contacted`: imported state.
- `Contact route verified`: a public business route is verified.
- `Authorization requested`: a human approved and sent the rights request.
- `Proof approved`: a human approved the private proof.
- `Outreach approved`: a human approved the recipient and final message.
- `Sent`: a human or separately authorized system sent the message.
- `Replied`: a response exists in connected mailbox evidence.

## Stop conditions

- Identity evidence conflicts.
- The price lacks a comparable basis.
- Media rights are missing.
- The villa is an inventory class without an exact physical unit.
- The proposed 3D output invents unseen rooms or circulation.
- The next action needs paid credits, personal-contact access, outreach or publication.
